# Important: Google Ads Should NOT Be "20% of the Website Price"

I would separate **website development** from **advertising management**.

For example:

**Website:** €1,000

**Google Ads setup:** €200–300 one-time

**Google Ads management:** €100–300/month

**Advertising budget:** paid directly by the client to Google

So you might tell the client:

> Website: €1,000
> Google Ads setup: €200
> Google Ads management: €100/month
> Google advertising budget: €300-500/month, paid directly to Google.

This is much cleaner than saying Google Ads costs 20% of the website.

# Domain & Hosting

You can also include:

**Domain:** included for the first year

**Hosting:** included for the first year or billed separately.

Then after the first year you can charge an annual maintenance/hosting fee.

For example:

> Website maintenance & hosting: €150/year

This creates **recurring revenue**, instead of making money only once from the website.
